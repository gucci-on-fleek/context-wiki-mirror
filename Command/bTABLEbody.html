Summary

The environment \bTABLEbody ... \eTABLEbody is used to delimit the body of a “natural table”, to separate it from (repeated) head and foot.

Settings

\bTABLEbody[...=...,...] ... \eTABLEbody
...=...,... inherits from \setupTABLE
